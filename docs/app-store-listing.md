## Description

Keep track of what you watch and what comes next.

Next Episode brings your TV shows, movies and viewing progress into one personal library.

YOUR LIBRARY
Search for shows and movies, discover popular titles and save the ones you want to follow.

YOUR WATCH PROGRESS
Mark episodes, seasons and movies as watched. See the episodes you still have to watch, and choose whether to mark earlier episodes when you catch up.

YOUR EPISODE CALENDAR
Browse past and upcoming episode dates. Scroll through your schedule and return to the current week with Today.

OPTIONAL REMINDERS
Choose reminders before or after an episode's scheduled start time. Notifications depend on available schedule information, device permissions and operating system delivery.

SHOW AND EPISODE DETAILS
Explore descriptions, seasons, cast and trailers where available. Find viewing services listed for a title; availability varies by country and service.

NO ACCOUNT REQUIRED
Your library and watch progress are stored on your device. The app is free, with no advertising or paid subscriptions. An internet connection is needed to search and update catalog information. Previously downloaded details can be used while available in the local cache.

Next Episode is a tracking app. It does not stream movies or TV episodes.

This application uses TMDB and the TMDB APIs but is not endorsed, certified, or otherwise approved by TMDB. Watch availability information is supplied by JustWatch through TMDB.

## App Review notes

No account or sign-in is required. Search for a show or movie and add it to the library to explore watch progress and the calendar. Optional episode notifications can be configured in Settings. The app uses TMDB for catalog information and does not provide video streaming. Library and watched status are stored locally on the device.
